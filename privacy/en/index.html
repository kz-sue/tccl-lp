Privacy Policy

日本語

ZOELAB ("we", "us" or "our") sets out below how we handle user information in the application "TaskChute Cloud Lite" (the "App") that we provide and on the App's official website at https://tccl.zoelab.jp/ (the "Site"). This document is referred to as the "Policy". Sections 1 to 7 apply to the App, and Section 9 applies to the Site.

1. Information We Collect

(1) Account information (only when linked to TaskChute Cloud 2)

The App obtains the following information only when you link it to (sign in with) a TaskChute Cloud 2 account. If you do not link an account, no account information is obtained.

  • Email address
  • Display name and profile image URL (provided by Google or Apple when you sign in with a Google account or Apple ID)
  • An identifier used for authentication (user ID)
  • The device's time zone and language settings (when registering a new account)

(2) Information you create

Information you enter into the App, such as task names, estimated durations, start and end times, routine settings, section settings, comments (notes) and images attached to comments, is stored on your device. If the App is linked to a TaskChute Cloud 2 account, this information (including comments and attached images) is also stored on the TaskChute Cloud 2 servers in order to provide the service, and is synchronized with the web version and your other devices. If no account is linked, this information is never sent outside your device.

(3) Usage information

To improve the quality of the App, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect the following information.

  • App interaction events (information indicating the type of action performed, such as creating, starting or completing a task, or progress through the tutorial)
  • Statistical information indicating usage patterns (such as the approximate number of registered tasks or whether cloud sync is in use, rounded into ranges or categories)
  • Device information (such as OS type and version, device model and App version, as collected by Google Analytics for Firebase by default)
  • A non-personally-identifying identifier (the app instance ID, a random identifier assigned per device and used to aggregate usage statistics)

This information is sent to servers operated by Google LLC and processed in accordance with Google's privacy policy. We never send the content you enter, such as task names, comment text or email addresses, as part of this collection. We do not collect advertising identifiers (AAID / IDFA), and we do not perform ad tracking or any other processing for advertising purposes. In Google's Consent Mode, only analytics storage (analytics_storage) is tied to your consent settings; the advertising-related settings (ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization_signals) are always set to "denied".

How usage information is collected depends on your region, as follows.

  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland — On devices determined to be located in one of these regions based on the device locale (country/region setting) or time zone, the App displays a screen on first launch asking for your consent to the collection of usage information. No usage information is collected until you give consent (this includes automatically collected information such as the first-open event). You can use all features of the App even if you do not consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time under "Settings → App Info → Share usage data". If you do not consent, or if you withdraw consent, the App stops collecting usage information and erases the analytics data stored on your device (such as the app instance ID). You can give consent again later from the same setting.
  • Users in all other regions — Collection of usage information is enabled by default. You can disable it at any time under "Settings → App Info → Share usage data".

(4) Purchase information (only when you purchase the Plus plan)

The App uses RevenueCat (provided by RevenueCat, Inc.) to manage the purchase status of in-app purchases (the Plus plan). The following information is sent to and stored on RevenueCat's servers.

  • An anonymous App User ID (an identifier issued by RevenueCat for each device; if the App is linked to a TaskChute Cloud 2 account, it is associated with that user ID)
  • Purchase and subscription status (such as the active plan, validity period and free-trial usage)
  • Transaction identifiers and receipt information issued by the store (App Store / Google Play)

Payment details such as credit card numbers are processed by Apple or Google and are never passed to us or to RevenueCat. The "Support ID" shown on the App's settings screen is this App User ID.

(5) Location information

The App does not obtain or collect location information. On iOS, a permission description relating to location may be displayed. This is due to a technical requirement of the library used to determine the time zone and does not mean that location information is obtained. Google Analytics for Firebase may infer an approximate location at the country or region level from your IP address, but this does not use the device's location features.

2. Purposes of Use

We use the information we collect for the following purposes.

  • To provide and maintain the App and its synchronization with TaskChute Cloud 2
  • To authenticate and verify the identity of users (including sending one-time codes for signing in)
  • To verify and restore the purchase status of the Plus plan
  • To improve the quality and features of the App
  • To investigate and respond to defects
  • To respond to inquiries (including checking purchase status by Support ID)

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services for cloud synchronization, authentication, management of in-app purchases and usage analytics. For details on how these services handle information, please refer to each service's privacy policy or similar documents.

  • TaskChute Cloud 2 (operated by jMatsuzaki, Inc.) — When an account is linked, used to synchronize and store account information and the information you create.
    TaskChute Cloud 2 Terms of Service

    Google Privacy Policy / Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Sign in with Apple (provided by Apple Inc., iOS version only)

  • RevenueCat (provided by RevenueCat, Inc.) — Used to manage, verify and restore the purchase status of the Plus plan.
    RevenueCat Privacy Policy

The App does not use any advertising services.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not disclose personal information to third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

5. Security Measures

Data transmitted between the App and servers (TaskChute Cloud 2 and third-party services) is encrypted using TLS. We take necessary and appropriate measures to prevent the leakage, loss or damage of the information we handle and to otherwise keep it secure.

6. Use of Device Features and Permissions

The App uses the following device features. None of them is used without your permission.

  • Notifications — Used to notify you of a task's scheduled start time, when an estimated duration has been exceeded, and when a section changes. All notifications are generated on the device (local notifications); we do not send push notifications.
  • Camera — Used to take photos and attach them to comments.
  • Photo library and files — Used to select images and attach them to comments.

7. Retention and Deletion of Information

  • Information stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • Information synchronized with TaskChute Cloud 2 remains on the servers even after you sign out of or uninstall the App. If you wish to delete the information on the servers, please close your account through TaskChute Cloud 2 (web version). See the Account Deletion page for instructions.
  • If you are unable to complete the account closure procedure, please contact us at the address below.

8. Information for Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the following applies in addition to the preceding sections, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection.

(1) Controller

The controller of your personal data in connection with the App is ZOELAB. Contact: contact@zoelab.jp

(2) Legal bases for processing

  • Processing of account information, synchronization and storage of the information you create, authentication, and purchase information — necessary for the performance of our contract with you (the App's terms of service) (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Processing of usage information (Google Analytics for Firebase) — your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
  • Processing to investigate and respond to defects, to respond to inquiries and to maintain security — our legitimate interest in providing the App safely and reliably (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)
  • Processing of usage information on the Site through Google Analytics (Section 9) — our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the use of the Site (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may stop this collection at any time as described in Section 9(3).

(3) Your rights

Under applicable law, you have the right to request access to, rectification or erasure of, or restriction of the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, the right to object to processing, and the right to withdraw consent. You can exercise these rights as follows.

  • To withdraw your consent to the collection of usage information, use "Settings → App Info → Share usage data" in the App (see Section 1(3)).
  • To erase information stored on the TaskChute Cloud 2 servers, close your account through TaskChute Cloud 2 (web version) (see Section 7 and the Account Deletion page).
  • For any other request, please contact us at the address above. We may ask you for additional information in order to verify your identity.

(4) Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.

(5) International data transfers

  • TaskChute Cloud 2 is operated under Japanese law by jMatsuzaki, Inc., a company incorporated in Japan. Japan has been recognized by the European Commission and the United Kingdom government as a country that ensures an adequate level of protection for personal data (adequacy decision).
  • Services provided by Google LLC (Firebase, Google Sign-In, Google Analytics), Apple Inc. (Sign in with Apple) and RevenueCat, Inc. may process data outside your region, including in the United States. These providers transfer data on the basis of legally recognized safeguards such as standard contractual clauses (SCCs). For the safeguards applied by Google, see Google's data transfer frameworks.

(6) Retention periods

Information is retained as described in Section 7. In addition, usage data sent to Google Analytics for Firebase that is associated with an individual app instance is retained for two months in accordance with the settings of our Firebase project, and is deleted thereafter. If you withdraw consent, the data on your device is erased immediately; data sent before withdrawal is deleted upon expiry of the retention period above. Data sent to Google Analytics from the Site that is associated with an individual visitor (cookie) is retained for two months in accordance with the settings of our Google Analytics property, and is deleted thereafter.

9. Use of Cookies and Similar Technologies on the Site

The Site uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand how the Site is used and to improve its content. Google Analytics uses cookies (small text files stored in your browser) and similar technologies to collect the following information.

(1) Information collected

  • The URL and title of the pages you view, the date and time of viewing, time spent on the Site, and the site that referred you to the Site
  • Browser and OS type and version, device category, screen size and language settings
  • An approximate location at the country or region level inferred from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored by Google Analytics)
  • On the store redirection page (the destination of the QR code on the Site), the type of store you are redirected to (App Store / Google Play) and the type of referral source

This information is collected anonymously, and we do not combine it with your name, email address or any other information that identifies you. The Site does not use the advertising features of Google Analytics (such as remarketing or advertising reports), and we do not serve ads or share data for advertising purposes.

(2) Purposes of use

  • Analyzing how the Site is viewed and improving its content and structure
  • Measuring the effectiveness of announcements and information about the App

(3) How to stop the collection

If you do not wish Google Analytics to collect information, install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on provided by Google, or disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies does not affect your ability to view the Site.

(4) How Google handles the information

Information collected through Google Analytics is managed in accordance with Google's privacy policy. For details, see the following.

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics Terms of Service

The Site does not use cookies for any purpose other than Google Analytics, and the Site does not ask you to enter any information.
